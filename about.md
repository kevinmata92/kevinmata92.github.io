I'm Kevin Mata, a security engineer with a background in cloud security, automation, Kubernetes, vulnerability management, and incident response.

I've spent much of my career building and securing cloud native systems, automating security workflows, and looking for ways to identify problems before they become incidents. My work has included everything from Python-based security automation and vulnerability remediation to container security, Zero Trust, and integrating security earlier into the development lifecycle.

This blog is where I plan on documenting any of my security research, vulnerabilities I discover, technical experiments, tooling, and lessons learned along the way. My goal is to share the some of the work and hopefully provide something useful to other security engineers and researchers.

When I'm not investigating security issues, I'm usually experimenting with automation, infrastructure, or listening to some house and techno /insert kid at club dancing meme.

All research published here is performed with the intent of responsible disclosure and improving the security of the systems involved.
